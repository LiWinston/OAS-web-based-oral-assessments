# Sprint 2 Retrospective

## Sprint Overview

**Duration:** Week 5 - 8 (30/03/2025 - 20/04/2025)

## 🌟 What Went Well

1. **Problem Solving Following Client Meeting Feedback**
   - Detailed meeting minutes documentation facilitates team members' review and clear understanding of the current project status
   - Discussions about updated requirements among team members and mentors, which clarified our understanding of the requirements
   - Promptly resolving requirement issues through email communications with the client after the client meeting
3. **Documentation Quality**
   - Visually appealing and intuitive high-fidelity prototypes that enhance user experience
   - Clear architecture diagrams that effectively show the system structure
   - Extensive documentations that present both the system design and solution
4. **Github Process Control**
   - Standardized Pull Requests before merging help the team conduct reviews and prevent most merge errors
   - Team members demonstrated strong engagement during the Pull Request process, with everyone actively participating in reviews and offering their suggestions
   - Well-structured issue tracker clearly shows the current progress of each part of project

## 🔄 What Could Be Improved

- Avoid misunderstanding of requirements and a lot of rework due to untimely communication with the client
- Improve time control for each discussion topics of team meetings
- All members can try to express their perspectives during planning and retrospective sessions of team meetings

## 🎯 Action Items for Next Sprint

- Have more active client communication outside of email to prevent misunderstanding of requirements and rework
- Have pre-meeting agenda and time boxing for discussion topics to improve meeting efficiency
- We can implement structured participation techniques during planning and retrospective sessions of team meeting to ensure all team members' perspectives are heard
