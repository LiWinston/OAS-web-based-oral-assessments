| Interaction with the LMS using CSV files may lead to various issues.<br><br>• Without detailed specification of the required CSV formats, when importing student data CSV files into the system, they might not be read correctly. Exporting assessment results back to Canvas might face formatting or compatibility issues.<br><br>• Student data imported from Canvas may not update properly if changes occur in LMS. | 0.7 | 0.7 | 0.49 | Avoid<br><br>• Confirm the CSV format with the client in advance to avoid problems during the development phase.<br><br>• Ensure that after importing CSV from the LMS, student information will not be updated further to avoid data inconsistency issues. |
| Data privacy and security concerns may arise with storing sensitive assessment information.<br><br>• Students' private information in the system being accessed by people who should not see it.<br><br>• CSV files may be leaked during the import and export process. | 0.6 | 0.9 | 0.54 | Mitigate<br><br>• Implement strict management of system permissions.<br><br>• Ensure that the import process can only be performed by administrators or authorized personnel. |
