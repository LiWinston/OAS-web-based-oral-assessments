## Discussion Points

Ian presented concerns about project coordination challenges from the previous semester and proposed mandatory process improvements. ### 2. Team Restructuring

The team addressed resource allocation issues and role optimization. ### 3. Technical Architecture Debate

Extended discussion regarding architectural approach for the remaining development timeline. Ian advocated for Next.js full-stack implementation to eliminate CORS issues and reduce iteration time. ### 4. Sprint Methodology Changes

The team evaluated current two-week sprint cycles and discussed optimization for the remaining ten-week timeline. Decision made to implement one-week sprint cycles to improve development velocity and reduce scope expansion risks.
