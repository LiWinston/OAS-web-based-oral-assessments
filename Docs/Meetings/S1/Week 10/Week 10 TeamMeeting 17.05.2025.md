## 2. Review of Sprint 3 and Planning for Final Project Wrap-up

The team discussed the outcomes of Sprint 3 and outlined the objectives for the final project phase, focusing on completing deliverables and preparing for the final presentation.

- **Sprint 3 Retrospective Summary:**

  - **Backend:**
    - Implementation progress was low and dependent on frontend completion.
    - Documentation (Sequence Diagrams, Use Cases) showed better progress, mostly in "In Review" or "Done".
    - Workload imbalance identified: Potentially too many backend developers for available tasks, or tasks misaligned. Some backend tasks pulled into Sprint 3 were deemed premature.
  - **Frontend:**
    - Prototype refinement (Examiner, Course Admin, Super Admin UI) had a higher completion rate and was mostly "Done".
    - Significant finalization still required for frontend components.
  - **General:** Overall sprint completion rate was low. Resource allocation and task dependencies were key challenges.

- **Final Project Phase Overview (No Sprint 4):**
  - **Goal:** Wrap up all remaining development, package deliverables (especially frontend for demo), and prepare for the final presentation.
  - The team will not proceed with a formal Sprint 4.
