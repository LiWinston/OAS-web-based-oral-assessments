# Stakeholder Analysis 

## Primary Stakeholders

### 1. Students

- Interests:

  - Easy booking of assessment slots
- Clear visibility of group members
  - Ability to reschedule bookings when needed
  - Timely access to marks and feedback
  - Privacy and data protection
  
- **Influence**: Medium

- Impact on Project: High

  - Students are the end-users who will directly interact with the booking system
- Their satisfaction levels will be a key measure of success
  - Their privacy concerns must be addressed through proper data handling

### 2. Examiners (Tutors)

- Interests:

  - Straightforward marking interface
- Clear schedule of assessments
  - Ability to provide structured feedback
  - Independent assessment process
  - Time-efficient marking system
  
- Influence: High

  - Examiners will provide direct input on usability of marking interface
- Their adoption of the system is critical for successful implementation
  
- Impact on Project: High

  - They will assess the effectiveness of the rubric and feedback system
- Their ability to efficiently mark will determine system success

### 3. Administrators (Subject Coordinators)

- Interests:

  - Streamlined data management
- Customizable assessment setup
  - Control over marks release
  - Accurate reporting
  - Integration with Canvas
  
- Influence: Very High

  - Primary decision-makers for system implementation
- Define system requirements and configurations
  
- Impact on Project: Very High

  - Will use the system to manage the entire assessment lifecycle
- Responsible for configuring assessment parameters

### 4. Client (Hasti Samadi, CIS Melbourne)

- Interests:

  - System that meets all specified requirements
- Reliable and scalable solution
  - Good user experience for all stakeholders
  - Successful implementation within timeline
  
- Influence: Very High

  - Final authority on project acceptance
- Can request changes to requirements
  
- Impact on Project: High

  - Will determine if project deliverables meet expectations
- Key decision-maker for project sign-off

## Secondary Stakeholders

### 5. University IT Department

- Interests:

  - System security and compliance
- Integration with existing university systems
  - Maintenance requirements
  - Technical standards adherence
  
- Influence: Medium

  - May influence technical architecture decisions
- Responsible for ongoing support
  
- Impact on Project: Medium

  - Will need to approve any integration with university systems
- May provide technical constraints or standards

### 6. Faculty Administration

- Interests:

  - System alignment with faculty policies
- Streamlined administrative processes
  - Reliable data for academic records
  
- Influence: Medium

  - May impose policy requirements
- May provide administrative constraints
  
- Impact on Project: Medium

  - Their processes may influence system design
- Need their cooperation for wider adoption

### 7. University Privacy Officer

- Interests:

  - Compliance with privacy regulations
- Protection of student and staff data
  - Appropriate consent mechanisms
  
- Influence: Medium

  - Can enforce privacy requirements
- May review system before approval
  
- Impact on Project: Medium

  - Must ensure system meets privacy standards
- Privacy requirements will affect data handling

### 8. Canvas LMS Support Team

- Interests:

  - Proper integration with Canvas
- Data format compatibility
  - Support requirements
  
- Influence: Low to Medium

  - May provide technical constraints for integration
- Limited direct influence on project decisions
  
- Impact on Project: Medium

  - Critical for successful data exchange with Canvas
- Integration requirements will affect system design

## Tertiary Stakeholders

### 9. Future System Maintainers

- Interests:

  - Well-documented code
- Maintainable architecture
  - Clear system design
  
- Influence: Low

  - Not directly involved in current development

- Impact on Project: Low to Medium

  - Will be responsible for long-term maintenance
- System sustainability depends on their ability to maintain it

### 10. Other Academic Departments

- Interests:

  - Potential adoption for their own assessments
- Compatibility with their processes
  
- Influence: Low

  - Not directly involved in current project

- Impact on Project: Low

  - May become future users
- Could influence long-term feature requirements

## Stakeholder Management Strategy

### High Priority Stakeholders (Students, Examiners, Administrators, Client)

- Regular consultations and updates
- Involvement in user acceptance testing
- Gather feedback on interface design and user experience
- Clear communication about project milestones and deliverables

### Medium Priority Stakeholders (IT Department, Faculty Administration, Privacy Officer)

- Regular updates on project progress
- Consultation on specific aspects relevant to their domains
- Early involvement to identify potential issues or constraints

### Low Priority Stakeholders (Future Maintainers, Other Departments)

- Documentation of decisions and rationales
- Consideration of future extensibility in design decisions
- Occasional updates on major project developments
