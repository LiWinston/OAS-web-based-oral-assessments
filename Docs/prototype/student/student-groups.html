Frequently Asked Questions

How are groups formed?

Groups are typically formed based on course coordinator assignments or self-selection, depending on the course requirements. Check your course syllabus for specific details.

Who can book assessment slots for group assignments?

Any member of the group can book an assessment slot, which will automatically apply to all group members. Once booked, the slot cannot be changed within 24 hours of the appointment.

What happens if a group member can't attend an assessment?

If a group member can't attend, they should notify their course coordinator as soon as possible. The attendance policy varies by course, so check your course guidelines.
