Privacy Policy & Data Consent

Please read and agree to the terms to continue using the Oral Assessment System.

1. Introduction

Welcome to the University of Melbourne's Oral Assessment System (OAS). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in relation to your use of this system. Your privacy is important to us.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Your name, student ID, and university email address (provided during registration or imported from university systems).
  • Information about your course enrollment and group memberships relevant to oral assessments.
  • Your assessment bookings (dates, times, locations).
  • Marks, grades, and feedback provided by examiners for your oral assessments.
  • System usage data (e.g., login times, actions performed) for security and auditing purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the purposes of administering oral assessments, including:

  • Managing your user account and authenticating your access.
  • Facilitating the booking and scheduling of assessment slots.
  • Allowing examiners to record marks and feedback.
  • Enabling you to view your assessment results and feedback.
  • Communicating important information regarding your assessments (e.g., confirmations, reminders).
  • Aggregating anonymized data for reporting and system improvement purposes.
  • Ensuring system security and compliance with university policies.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel (students, assigned examiners, system administrators) based on their roles.

5. Data Sharing

Your assessment results (marks and feedback) may be exported by administrators for upload into the university's official Learning Management System (e.g., Canvas) as part of the formal grading process. We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing or other unrelated purposes.

6. Data Retention

Your data within the OAS will be retained in accordance with the University of Melbourne's records management policies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access and review the personal information held about you in this system. You can manage some of your information through the Settings page. For further inquiries or requests regarding your data, please contact the system administrator or the relevant university privacy office.

8. Consent

By clicking "Agree and Continue", you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and use of your personal information as described herein for the purpose of managing your oral assessments through the OAS.
