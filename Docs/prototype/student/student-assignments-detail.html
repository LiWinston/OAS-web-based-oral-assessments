Detailed Description

This assessment requires each group to present their final software project developed throughout the semester for SWEN90017. The presentation should cover the following key aspects:

  • System architecture and key design decisions.
  • Demonstration of core features and functionality.
  • Discussion of technologies used and rationale.
  • Challenges faced and how they were overcome.
  • Potential future improvements.

The presentation should be approximately 15 minutes long, followed by a 5-minute Q&A session with the examiners. All group members are expected to participate in the presentation.

Please ensure your presentation slides are prepared in advance. You will have access to a projector and standard AV equipment in the assessment room.

Marking Rubric Overview

The assessment will be marked based on the following criteria:

  • Clarity and completeness of the system demonstration (30%)
  • Soundness of architectural design and justification (30%)
  • Quality of presentation and communication (20%)
  • Individual contribution and understanding (during Q&A) (10%)
  • Professionalism and teamwork (10%)

A detailed rubric will be used by the examiners during the assessment. Ensure your presentation addresses these key areas.
